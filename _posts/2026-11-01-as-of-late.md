<img src="/assets/img/posts/asoflate/one.jpg" width="100%">

I haven't really liked being in my apartment for a while now. 

<img src="/assets/img/posts/asoflate/two.jpg" width="100%">

It feels like the mark of a particularly acrid soul to be so miserable about
other people enjoying each other's company. It feels terrible to feel this way.
I hate being a hateful lump of coal. It feels as though I've hammered on all
this steel around a heart that was already bursting at the seams and now I
feel as though my chest may collapse from the slight pressure of sunlight. 

<img src="/assets/img/posts/asoflate/three.jpg" width="100%">

It was a beautiful day today and I couldn't enjoy it. You can't always
get what you want.
